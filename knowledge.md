## 1. ActivateMe® Fest — the basics

- **What it is:** Dubai's first-of-its-kind family community festival, where movement, creativity
  and discovery come together. Kids and adults try sports, music, dance, tech, gaming, arts and
  hands-on activities, and parents connect with academies and clubs.
- **Edition:** Season 2. Season 1 (the inaugural festival) took place in January 2026.
- **Dates:** Saturday 16 and Sunday 17 January 2027 (two days).
- **Venue:** Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO), Digital Park, Dubai, UAE.
  Google Maps: https://maps.app.goo.gl/5NCoacxb1tqsB6qy5
- **Organiser:** MWAN Events (https://www.mwanevents.com/)
- **Website:** https://www.activatemefest.com/
- **Who it's for:** Families — children of all ages, teens, parents and adults. Designed to be
  inclusive and accessible for all ages, abilities and backgrounds.
- **Big idea:** Balance between fun and growth, play and learning, movement and creativity.
  "Not just entertainment — a launchpad." Kids discover interests they can keep doing after the
  festival, with academies and clubs they meet on the day.

## 7. Visitor FAQs

- **Parking:** Yes — designated car parks are available at the venue.
- **Kids:** Children under 16 must be accompanied by a responsible adult at all times. Kids must
  wear sport shoes at all times for safety and comfort.
- **People of Determination / accessibility:** All People of Determination are welcome; the team
  is committed to an inclusive, enjoyable experience for everyone.
- **Smoking:** Only in designated outdoor areas. Please use the bins provided.
- **Camping:** Not permitted.
- **Photography & filming:** The event will be photographed and filmed. By attending, you consent
  to your image being used in future ActivateMe™ Fest and MWAN Events promotional material.
- **Lost & found:** Located near the main entrance. Lost something or found something? Go to any
  Information Point.
- **Safety:** Medical stations throughout the venue, clearly marked emergency exits, security
  on-site.
- **What to bring:** Valid government-issued photo ID, comfortable sport shoes,
  weather-appropriate clothing, portable phone charger, reusable water bottle, a small backpack or
  bag, and cash and cards for vendors.
- **Not allowed:** Illegal items or substances; glass (bottles, containers, other glass items);
  outside food or drinks (except sealed water bottles or for medical needs); fireworks, flares or
  explosives; drones or other aerial devices (visitors' own); laser pointers; pets (service
  animals are welcome).
- **Tips:** Stay hydrated and take breaks.
